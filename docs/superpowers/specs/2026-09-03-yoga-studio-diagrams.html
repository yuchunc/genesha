Solo yoga studio · Taiwan · teacher-only phase 1 · derived from the August 上課、調課、補課名單 document

Domain model

Hover an entity to isolate its relationships. Attendance is the only table that puts a name on a date — it is the roster row, and every kind of participation flows through it.

Schedule Booking People Entitlement Money dashed = event-driven, not structural
  • Why session.style exists. Her doc writes *基礎8/26 on the Wednesday 流動 slot and *流動8/28 on the Friday 基礎 slot. Style belongs to the date, not the slot.
  • Why price lives on the enrollment. 1600/4堂, 1200/3堂, 900 for 2堂, 800 for 2堂 and 450 單堂 all coexist in one month. A plain integer entered per enrollment beats any discount engine.
  • Why payment is keyed to (student, month). The question she asks is monthly. One NT$2,400 transfer covering two slots then needs no allocation table; enrollment_id stays as a display hint so her per-slot layout survives.
  • Why 調課 and 補課 share one mechanism. Both are kind: :makeup consuming a credit. 宜群's 調8/21 and 蘭子's 補7/10颱風假 differ only in credit.source.

Credit lifecycle

Two sources, one consumption mechanism, two different lifespans. This replaces the hand-maintained 備註：蘭子補課8/17 or 8/31 line.

  • Portability is real, not theoretical. 蘭子 earned a Friday cancellation credit and could spend it on Monday 8/17 or 8/31 — so credits are held by the student, never by the slot.
  • Why package credits expire but cancellation credits don't. 宜群 swapped 8/21→8/17 inside August, while July's 颱風假 credits were still being spent in August. The asymmetry is her actual behaviour.

Payment trust boundary

No API in Taiwan can tell this app that money landed. The bot captures a claim; only a human can assert it is true. That boundary is enforced by the schema, not by a comment.

  • Manual entry may go straight to :confirmed — she is the human, and for cash she already knows. The invariant is that a human action is required, not that a claim step happened.
  • Barter needs a nullable amount. 玉珊 and 欽玉 both paid by 按摩器代購. Forcing a number there would be inventing revenue.
  • Future :ecpay source could self-confirm. A webhook-verified payment is machine-provable, so one ledger can hold two source types at two trust levels — LINE claims stay human-confirmed.
