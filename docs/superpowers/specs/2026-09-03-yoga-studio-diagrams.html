Ganesha — Studio Ledger Design

Solo yoga studio · Taiwan · teacher-only phase 1 · derived from the August 上課、調課、補課名單 document

Domain model

Hover an entity to isolate its relationships. Purchase is the money side of a sale; Attendance is the roster row that puts a name on a date. Splitting them is what lets one payment point at exactly one thing, and lets a free makeup exist with no sale behind it.

  • The model now explains her two different 2-class prices. 素容 paid 900 for 2堂 = 450×2, the drop-in rate. 彩華 paid 800 for 2堂 = 400×2, the package rate. As a flat price integer both were arbitrary numbers; as PACKAGE + session count they are structurally different sales, and which one she used becomes visible.
  • custom_amount instead of a discount field. Her doc records final agreed numbers — 400, 800, 900, 1200 — never deltas. She types what was agreed; the system never asks her to compute a difference. Adjustments can also go up, and "how much did I comp" is still derivable as list_price − payable.
  • 按摩器代購 is custom_amount = 0 plus a note, not a null amount and not a fake number. payment.amount stays NOT NULL — no nullable money in the schema.
  • No month column anywhere. A purchase's period is derived from the dates of its attendance rows. Cost: "August revenue" joins through attendance → session. Benefit: a purchase spanning a month boundary needs no special case, and there is no denormalised field to drift.
  • purchase_id is Payment's only foreign key. Because a drop-in is also a purchase, payment needs no nullable FK pair and no polymorphic association. Student is reached via the purchase.
  • attendance.purchase_id is nullable, and that is the point. A makeup has no sale behind it — it is paid for by a credit. 允一's 無費用，補颱風假 row is exactly this shape.
  • 調課 and 補課 share one mechanism. Both are kind: :makeup consuming a credit; 宜群's 調8/21 and 蘭子's 補7/10颱風假 differ only in credit.source.

Credit lifecycle

Two sources, one consumption mechanism, two different lifespans. This replaces the hand-maintained 備註：蘭子補課8/17 or 8/31 line.

  • Portability is real, not theoretical. 蘭子 earned a Friday cancellation credit and could spend it on Monday 8/17 or 8/31 — so credits are held by the student, never by the slot.
  • Why package credits expire but cancellation credits don't. 宜群 swapped 8/21→8/17 inside August, while July's 颱風假 credits were still being spent in August. The asymmetry is her actual behaviour.
  • The count is configuration, not a constant. included_makeups lives on the package: 1 for 月課程, 0 for 單堂 and 體驗. Changing the policy later is a data edit, not a migration.

Money flow — price, override, claim, confirm

The package suggests a number; she may override it outright. Payment is then a claim until a human confirms it, because no API in Taiwan can tell this app that money landed.

  • Why list_price is still stored even when overridden. It is the snapshot of what the package cost at sale time, so the price list can change without rewriting history — and it is what makes the override visible rather than silent.
  • custom_amount is freeform. Any integer, above or below list, zero included. No percentage rule, no rounding policy, no discount arithmetic to keep consistent.
  • Manual entry may go straight to :confirmed — she is the human, and for cash she already knows. The invariant is that a human action is required, not that a claim step happened.
  • Outstanding = Σ payable − Σ confirmed payments, per student, over any date range derived from attendance. Unconfirmed claims are deliberately excluded, so "who still owes me" never counts money she hasn't actually seen.
  • A future :ecpay source could self-confirm. A webhook-verified payment is machine-provable, so one ledger holds two source types at two trust levels — LINE claims stay human-confirmed.
